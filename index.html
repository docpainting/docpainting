Boston & South Shore's Premier Painters: Artistry, Innovation & A Family Promise

Based in Holbrook, MA, DOC Painting is where master craftsmanship meets cutting-edge technology. We're a family-owned, award-winning business with a passion for transforming spaces across the Boston South Shore, Norfolk, and Plymouth County. We don't just paint walls; we bring your vision to life with color, precision, and a personal touch that only a dedicated family business can provide.

Our Specialized Craft & Innovation

We thrive on projects that demand creativity and expertise. Our team specializes in creating extraordinary environments using advanced techniques and pioneering technology:

  • High-End & Faux Finishes: Achieving stunning mirrored high-gloss effects with Fine Paints of Europe, elegant limewash, and custom faux finishes.
  • Expert Deck Restoration: Professional finishing for exotic woods like Brazilian Rosewood, using premium Penofin products for lasting beauty and protection.
  • Historical Restoration: Meticulously restoring the beauty and character of Victorian and historical homes with period-accurate detail.
  • Custom Art & Design: From 3D motivational artwork for businesses to unique patterned decks, we create pieces that tell a story.
  • The Future of Painting (R&D): We are pioneering the next generation of our craft by developing AI-powered drones. Our R&D initiative leverages machine learning, LLM-integrated depth cameras, and NVIDIA Jetson systems to create autonomous painting solutions that will redefine precision.

We believe in bold design. In fact, clients who embrace cutting-edge color trends receive a special discount!
